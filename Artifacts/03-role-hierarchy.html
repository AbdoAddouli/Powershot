Salesforce Role Hierarchy

The role hierarchy controls record visibility for objects with Private sharing model. Users higher in the hierarchy can view/edit records owned by users below them. This hierarchy works in conjunction with OWD settings, sharing rules, and permission sets.

Important: The role hierarchy grants visibility based on record ownership, not the permission set. A user with O_G_Executive (read-only) can still see records owned by users below them in the hierarchy — but they can only read them, not edit, because the permission set restricts CRUD. The hierarchy and permission sets work together: hierarchy controls who can see, permission sets control what they can do.
