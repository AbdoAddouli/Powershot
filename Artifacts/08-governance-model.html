Organizational Governance Structure

PowerShot governance is organized into four domains: Strategic Steering, Technical Governance, Operational Governance, and Security Governance. Each domain has clear ownership, decision rights, and escalation paths.

Steering Committee
Executive Leadership
Strategic direction & budget
Technical Governance
Platform Admin + Dev Team
Architecture, code, deployments
Operational Governance
HSE Compliance Team
Incident management, compliance
Security Governance
System Admin
Access, sharing, data protection

Public Groups

Executive Leadership
Receives escalation emails for Critical-severity HSE incidents. Approves major platform changes and budget decisions.
Members: CEO, VPs, C-Suite executives
HSE Compliance Team
Receives regulatory notification emails for reportable HSE incidents. Monitors compliance report deadlines and permit renewals.
Members: HSE Advisors, Compliance Analysts, Regulatory staff
